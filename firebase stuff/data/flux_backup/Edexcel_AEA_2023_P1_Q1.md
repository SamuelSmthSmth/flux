### Question 1

**(a)** Write down the exact value of $\cos 405^\circ$ **(1)**

**(b)** Hence, using a double angle identity for cosine, or otherwise, determine the exact value of $\cos 101.25^\circ$, giving your answer in the form $a\sqrt{b + c\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}$ where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are rational numbers. **(5)**

### Mark Scheme 1

#### **Part (a)**

**Step 1: Exact value** $$\cos 405^\circ = \cos 45^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$ oe

- **[B1]** Correct value.
    

#### **Part (b)**

**Step 2: Apply double angle identity** $$\cos 2\theta = 2\cos^2\theta - 1 \Rightarrow \cos^2\theta = \frac{1}{2}(1 + \cos 2\theta)$$ with $\theta = A$ or $2A$ (where $A = 101.25^\circ$)

- **[M1]** Applies double angle identity to get $\cos A$ or its square in terms of $\cos 2A$, or $\cos 2A$ in terms of $\cos 4A$.
    

**Step 3: Expression for cos 2A** $$\Rightarrow \cos 2A = \pm\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)}$$

- **[M1]** Proceeds to find expression for $\cos 2A$, allow if sign not considered.
    

**Step 4: Determine correct sign for cos 2A** $$\cos 2A = -\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)}$$ As $2A$ is in third quadrant.

- **[A1]** Correct value for $\cos 2A$ (must have the negative and reject the positive).
    

**Step 5: Apply double angle identity again for cos A** $$\cos A = \pm\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}(1 + \cos 2A)} = \pm\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\left(1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)}\right)}$$

- **[M1]** Complete method to get the required cosine (ie second use of double angle formula).
    

**Step 6: Final Answer** $$\cos A = -\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)}\right)}$$ $A$ is obtuse so $$= -\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}$$

- **[A1]** Correct answer, accept any reasonably simplified answer in the form specified.
