### **Question 1**

**(a)** By writing $u = \log_{4}r$, where $r > 0$ show that $$\log_{4}r = \frac{1}{2}\log_{2}r$$ **(2)**

**(b)** Solve the equation $$\log_{4}(5x^{2} - 11) = \log_{2}(3x - 5)$$ **(5)**

### **Mark Scheme 1**

#### **Part (a)**

**Step 1: Convert base** $$u = \log_{4}r \Rightarrow 4^{u} = r$$ Taking logs to base 2: $$u \log_{2}4 = \log_{2}r \Rightarrow 2u = \log_{2}r$$

- **[M1]** Uses relationship between logs and indices or change of base formula.
    

**Step 2: Final Result** $$\Rightarrow u = \log_{4}r = \frac{\log_{2}r}{2}$$

- **[A1]** Correct work leading to the given answer.
    

#### **Part (b)**

**Step 3: Make bases consistent** $$\frac{1}{2}\log_{2}(5x^{2} - 11) = \log_{2}(3x - 5)$ or $\log_{2}(5x^{2} - 11) = 2\log_{2}(3x - 5)$$

- **[M1, M1]** Uses part (a) to get both terms to the same base, and applies power rule.
    

**Step 4: Remove logarithms** $$\Rightarrow 5x^{2} - 11 = (3x - 5)^{2}$$

- **[M1]** Removes the logs (must involve a power).
    

**Step 5: Form and solve quadratic** $$\Rightarrow 5x^{2} - 11 = 9x^{2} - 30x + 25$$ $$\Rightarrow 4x^{2} - 30x + 36 = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad 2x^{2} - 15x + 18 = 0$$ $$\Rightarrow (2x - 3)(x - 6) = 0$$

- **[M1]** Forms and solves a three-term quadratic.
    

**Step 6: Identify valid solutions** $$x = \frac{3}{2} \ \ \text{or} \ \ 6$$

- **[A1]** Both answers found.
    

**Step 7: Check domains** Noting that $3x - 5 < 0$ when $x = \frac{3}{2}$ (so log term is undefined) and hence the only valid solution is $x = 6$.

- **[B1]** Single final answer with valid reason.
