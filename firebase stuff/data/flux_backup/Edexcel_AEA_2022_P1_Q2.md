### Question 2

**Figure 1** _(A diagram shows a regular hexagon OPQRST. Vector p points from O to P. Vector q points from O to Q.)_

Figure 1 shows a regular hexagon $OPQRST$. The vectors $\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ are defined by $\mathbf{p} = \overrightarrow{OP}$ and $\mathbf{q} = \overrightarrow{OQ}$.

Forces, in Newtons, $F_P = \overrightarrow{OP}$, $F_Q = 2 \times \overrightarrow{OQ}$, $F_R = 3 \times \overrightarrow{OR}$, $F_S = 4 \times \overrightarrow{OS}$ and $F_T = 5 \times \overrightarrow{OT}$ are applied to a particle.

**(a)** Find, in terms of $\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q}$, the resultant force on the particle. **(5)**

The magnitude of the acceleration of the particle due to these forces is $13 \text{ ms}^{-2}$. Given that the mass of the particle is $3 \text{ kg}$, **(b)** find $|\mathbf{p}|$. **(5)**

### Mark Scheme 2

#### **Part (a)**

**Step 1: Find individual vectors** E.g. centre point is $$\overrightarrow{PQ} = \overrightarrow{OQ} - \overrightarrow{OP}$$ so $$\overrightarrow{OR} = 2 \times (\overrightarrow{OQ} - \overrightarrow{OP}); \quad \overrightarrow{OS} = \overrightarrow{OQ} - 2\overrightarrow{OP} + \overrightarrow{PQ}; \quad \overrightarrow{OT} = \overrightarrow{OQ} - 2\overrightarrow{OP}$$

- **[M1]** Attempts to find at least one of the other vectors in terms of $\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ with at least one correct expression.
    

**Step 2: Correct expressions for vectors** $$\overrightarrow{OR} = 2(\mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}); \quad \overrightarrow{OS} = 2\mathbf{q} - 3\mathbf{p}; \quad \overrightarrow{OT} = \mathbf{q} - 2\mathbf{p}$$

- **[A1, A1]** Two correct; all three correct.
    

**Step 3: Sum the forces** $$R = \mathbf{p} + 2\mathbf{q} + 3 \times 2(\mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}) + 4(2\mathbf{q} - 3\mathbf{p}) + 5(\mathbf{q} - 2\mathbf{p}) = \dots$$

- **[M1]** Attempts the sum using their expressions with at least one force involving both $\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q}$.
    

**Step 4: Resultant Force** $= -27\mathbf{p} + 21\mathbf{q}$

- **[A1]** Correct answer.
    

#### **Part (b)**

**Step 5: Strategy for magnitude** E.g. $R = 21(\mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}) - 6\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{p}$ have same length, say $p$ (regular hexagon sides), with angle $60^\circ$ between.

- **[M1]** Formulates a correct strategy - may be other ways, e.g. finding length of $\mathbf{q}$ relative to $\mathbf{p}$ first.
    

**Step 6: Apply Cosine Rule** So $|R|^2 = (6p)^2 + (21p)^2 - 2(6p)(21p)\cos 60^\circ$ $$(= (3p)^2(2^2 + 7^2 - 2 \times 7) = (3p)^2 \times 39)$$

- **[M1]** Applies cosine rule to appropriate triangle.
    

**Step 7: Magnitude squared** $$|R|^2 = 39(3p)^2 \quad \text{or} \quad |R| = 3p\sqrt{39}$$

- **[A1]** Correct simplified magnitude or its square.
    

**Step 8: Apply Newton's Second Law** $$|F| = m|a| \Rightarrow 3p\sqrt{39} = 3 \times 13 \Rightarrow p = \dots$$

- **[M1]** Applies $|F| = m|a|$ to find $p$. Must include attempt at modulus.
    

**Step 9: Final Answer** $$p = \frac{13}{\sqrt{39}} = \frac{\sqrt{39}}{3}$$

- **[A1]** Correct answer, allow either form.
