### Question 1

**Figure 1** _(A sketch of a curve with a central inverted U-shape having a maximum at point P. The curve has vertical asymptotes at x=0 and x=a, and a horizontal asymptote at y=1. The outer branches approach the asymptotes from above.)_

Figure 1 shows a sketch of the curve with equation $y = f(x)$ where $$f(x) = 1 + \frac{4}{x(x - 3)}$$ The curve has a turning point at the point $P$, and the lines with equations $y = 1$, $x = 0$ and $x = a$ are asymptotes to the curve.

**(a)** Write down the value of $a$. **(1)**

**(b)** Find the coordinates of $P$, justifying your answer. **(4)**

**(c)** Sketch the curve with equation $$y = \left| f\left(x + \frac{3}{2}\right) \right| - 1$$ On your sketch, you should show the coordinates of any points of intersection with the coordinate axes, the coordinates of any turning points and the equations of any asymptotes. **(7)**

### Mark Scheme 1

#### **Part (a)**

**Step 1: Identify vertical asymptote** $a = 3$

- **[B1]** Correct value of $a$.
    

#### **Part (b)**

**Step 2: Differentiate** $$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{-4(2x - 3)}{(x^2 - 3x)^2}$$ (or e.g. $$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{4}{3x^2} - \frac{4}{3(x-3)^2}$$ oe)

- **[B1]** Correct derivative.
    

**Step 3: Find x-coordinate of P** $$\frac{dy}{dx} = 0 \Rightarrow x = \frac{3}{2}$$

- **[M1]** Attempts $\frac{dy}{dx} = 0$ to find $x$.
    
- **[A1]** Correct x-coordinate from a correct derivative.
    

**Step 4: Find y-coordinate of P** $$y = 1 - \frac{4}{\frac{3}{2}(\frac{3}{2} - 3)} = -\frac{7}{9}$$ so $P$ is $\left(\frac{3}{2}, -\frac{7}{9}\right)$

- **[A1]** Correct y-coordinate from a correct derivative.
