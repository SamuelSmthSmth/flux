### Question 1

In the binomial expansion of $$(1 - 8x)^p \quad |x| < \frac{1}{8}$$

where $p$ is a positive constant,

- the sum of the coefficient of $x$ and the coefficient of $x^2$ is equal to the coefficient of $x^3$
- the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive

Determine the value of $p$. **(7)**

### Mark Scheme

**Step 1: Expand the binomial** $$(1 - 8x)^p = (1+) -8px + \frac{p(p-1)}{2}(-8x)^2 + \frac{p(p-1)(p-2)}{6}(-8x)^3 + \dots$$

- **[B1]** Correct numerical coefficients of relevant terms, may be seen in an expansion or listed separately. Need not be simplified.
    

**Step 2: Equate coefficients** $$\Rightarrow -8p + \frac{64p(p-1)}{2} = -\frac{8^3 p(p-1)(p-2)}{6}$$

- **[M1]** Equates the relevant coefficients. If the $x$'s are left in and never recovers just coefficients, M0.
    

**Step 3: Simplify to a quadratic** $$\Rightarrow -3 + 12(p-1) = -32(p^2 - 3p + 2)$$

- **[dM1]** Cancels or factors to achieve a quadratic in $p$ (need not be expanded).
    

$$\Rightarrow 32p^2 - 84p + 49 = 0$$

- **[A1]** Correct expanded and gathered quadratic.
    

**Step 4: Solve the quadratic** $$\Rightarrow (4p - 7)(8p - 7) = 0 \Rightarrow p = \dots$$

- **[ddM1]** Solves their 3 term quadratic.
    

**Step 5: Use condition to disambiguate roots** Need $$p(p-1) > 0$ so ($p < 0$ or) $p > 1$$

- **[M1]** Correct consideration of the $x^2$ coefficient to disambiguate (a selection must be made). Must have had at least two roots to choose from.
    
- _Note:_ May be by substitution to see which gives a positive, or consideration of ranges. Note $p > 0$ stated is M0. If they have incorrect roots allow for due consideration given to attempt to select appropriate root(s).
    

**Step 6: Final Answer** $$p = \frac{7}{4}$$

- **[A1]** Correct answer only.
