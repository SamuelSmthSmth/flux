### Question 5

Figure 2 shows a sketch of a hexagon `OABCDE` where

- the interior angle at `O` and at `C` are each `60^\circ`
    
- the interior angle at each of the other vertices is `150^\circ`
    
- `OA = OE = BC = CD`
    
- `AB = ED = 3 \times OA`
    

Given that `\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}` and `\overrightarrow{OE} = \mathbf{e}`

determine as simplified expressions in terms of `\mathbf{a}` and `\mathbf{e}` **(a)** `\overrightarrow{AB}` **(3)** **(b)** `\overrightarrow{OD}` **(2)**

The point `R` divides `AB` internally in the ratio `1 : 2` **(c)** Determine `\overrightarrow{RC}` as a simplified expression in terms of `\mathbf{a}` and `\mathbf{e}` **(2)**

The line through the points `R` and `C` meets the line through the points `O` and `D` at the point `X`. **(d)** Determine `\overrightarrow{OX}` in the form `\lambda\mathbf{a} + \mu\mathbf{e}`, where `\lambda` and `\mu` are real values in simplest form. **(6)** **(+S2)**

### Mark Scheme

#### **Part (a)**

**Step 1: Recognise parallel vector** `\overrightarrow{AB} = k(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e})`

- **[B1]** Recognises `\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e}` is parallel to `\overrightarrow{AB}`
    

**Step 2: Compare lengths / modulus** `|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e}| = 2|\mathbf{a}|\cos 30^\circ = |\mathbf{a}|\sqrt{3}` `|\overrightarrow{AB}| = 3|\mathbf{a}| \Rightarrow k = \dots`

- **[M1]** Correct method for comparing modulus/working out length of `\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e}`
    

**Step 3: Final Answer** `\Rightarrow \overrightarrow{AB} = \sqrt{3}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e})`

- **[A1]** Correct answer (oe)
    

#### **Part (b)**

**Step 4: Form vector path** `\overrightarrow{OD} = \overrightarrow{OE} + \overrightarrow{ED} = \overrightarrow{OE} + \overrightarrow{AB} = \dots`

- **[M1]** A correct strategy to find `\overrightarrow{OD}`
    

**Step 5: Final Expression** `\overrightarrow{OD} = \mathbf{e} + \sqrt{3}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e}) = \sqrt{3}\mathbf{a} + (\sqrt{3} + 1)\mathbf{e}`

- **[A1]** Either form acceptable (oe).
    

#### **Part (c)**

**Step 6: Form vector path for `RC`** `\overrightarrow{RC} = \overrightarrow{RB} + \overrightarrow{BC} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{OE} = \dots`

- **[M1]** For any correct strategy to find `\overrightarrow{RC}`
    

**Step 7: Final Expression** `= \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{3}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e}) + \mathbf{e} = \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{3}\mathbf{a} + \left(\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{3} + 1\right)\mathbf{e}`

- **[A1]** A correct expression
    

#### **Part (d)**

**Step 8: First expression for `OX`** `\overrightarrow{OX} = \overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{AR} + p\overrightarrow{RC}` `= \mathbf{a} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{e}) + \frac{2p}{3}\sqrt{3}\mathbf{a} + p\left(\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{3} + 1\right)\mathbf{e}`

- **[M1, A1]** For any complete, valid method to obtain `\overrightarrow{OX}` in terms `\mathbf{a}`, `\mathbf{e}` and a scalar using the collinearity of `R`, `C` and `X`.
    

**Step 9: Second expression for `OX`** `\overrightarrow{OX} = q\overrightarrow{OD} = q\sqrt{3}\mathbf{a} + q(\sqrt{3} + 1)\mathbf{e}`

- **[B1ft]** For a second correct expression for `\overrightarrow{OX}` in terms of `\mathbf{a}`, `\mathbf{e}` and a scalar using their `\overrightarrow{OD}`.
    

> _Note: allow the first three marks if the same variable is used for both equations, but the next M will not be available unless they recover to two variables._

**Step 10: Equate coefficients and solve** `1 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} + \frac{2p\sqrt{3}}{3} = q\sqrt{3}` `\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} + p\left(\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} + 1\right) = q(\sqrt{3} + 1)` `(\Rightarrow p - 1 = q) \Rightarrow p = \dots \text{ or } q = \dots`

- **[M1]** Equates components of the two equations and solves. (S+)
    

**Step 11: Find scalar parameter** `p = \sqrt{3} + 4 \text{ or } q = \sqrt{3} + 3`

- **[A1]** A correct value for either of the two unknowns that were used.
    

**Step 12: Final simplified vector** `\overrightarrow{OX} = (3\sqrt{3} + 1)\mathbf{a} + (2\sqrt{3} + 3)\mathbf{e}`

- **[A1]** Correct answer. Accept equivalent simplified in correct form.
