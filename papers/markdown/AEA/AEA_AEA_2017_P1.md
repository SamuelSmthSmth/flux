### **Question 1**

The function $f$ is given by
$$f(x) = \sqrt{x+2} \quad \text{for } x \in \mathbb{R}, x \ge 0$$

**(a)** Find $f^{-1}(x)$ and state the domain of $f^{-1}$. **(2)**

**(b)** Find the range of $g$. **(2)**

**(c)** Solve the equation $fg(x) = x$. **(3)**

**(Total 7 marks)**

- **[B1]** Correct domain.

#### **Part (b)**

**Step 3: Complete square or differentiate to find minimum** $g(x) = (x - 2)^2 + 1$ (or differentiation or equivalent)

- **[A1]** Correct range.

#### **Part (c)**

- **[A1]** Correct value of $x$.

### **Question 3**

The line $L_1$ has equation $\mathbf{r} = \begin{pmatrix} -13 \\ 7 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} + t \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

**(3)**

**(Total 13 marks)**

**Step 1: Equate components for line intersection** Line $L_2$: $\mathbf{r} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ p \\ 10 \end{pmatrix} + s \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 11 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix}$.

$$1 - 2s = -13 + 6t \quad \text{and} \quad 10 - 5s = -1 + 3t$$

- **[M1]** Form two equations in $t$ and $s$.

**Step 2: Solve simultaneous equations**

$$14 - 2s = 6t \quad \text{and} \quad 22 - 10s = 6t \Rightarrow 8 - 8s = 0 \Rightarrow s = 1, \, t = 2$$

- **[M1]** Solving simultaneous equations.
- **[A1]** $s = 1, t = 2$.

**Step 3: Find p and position vector of B** $s = 1 \Rightarrow p = -8$; $t = 2 \Rightarrow \vec{OB} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix}$.

- **[A1]** $p = -8$.
- **[A1]** Position vector of $B$ is $\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix}$.

#### **Part (b)**

$$\vec{OC} = \begin{pmatrix} -13 + 6t \\ 7 - 2t \\ -1 + 3t \end{pmatrix} \Rightarrow \vec{AC} = \begin{pmatrix} 6t - 14 \\ -2t + 15 \\ 3t - 11 \end{pmatrix}$$

- **[M1]** Attempt $\vec{AC}$ in terms of $t$.

$$\vec{AC} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = 0$$

- **[dM1]** Intend correct scalar product equal to zero.

- **[dM1]** Solve equation in $t$.

**Step 7: Find position vector of C**

$$\vec{OC} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 1 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}$$

- **[A1]** Correct position vector of $C$.

#### **Part (c)**

**Step 8: Calculate lengths BC and AC**

$$|\vec{BC}| = \sqrt{6^2 + (-2)^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{49} = 7, \quad |\vec{AC}| = \sqrt{4^2 + 9^2 + (-2)^2} = \sqrt{101}$$

- **[M1]** Attempt at least one length.

- **[M1]** Correct method for area.

$$\text{Area} = 7\sqrt{101}$$

- **[A1]** Correct answer.

### **Question 4**

**Figure 1** _(An equilateral triangle $LMN$ of side $2\text{ cm}$ with points $P$ on $LM$ at distance $x\text{ cm}$ from $L$ and $Q$ on $LN$ at distance $y\text{ cm}$ from $L$, showing line segment $PQ$.)_

#### **Part (b)**

- **[M1]** Use of sector area formula.

- **[B1]** Complete argument.
